Our Process

Every bowl of ramen at ZUKU starts long before it reaches your table. We spend hours crafting our broth, carefully selecting fresh ingredients, and perfecting every detail — because great ramen takes time, and you deserve the best.

🍖

The Broth

Simmered for 12+ hours with carefully selected bones and aromatics to build a rich, deep flavor.

🌿

Fresh Ingredients

We source fresh vegetables and toppings daily to ensure every bowl is vibrant and full of life.

🍜

The Noodles

Perfectly cooked to order — springy, smooth, and ready to soak up every drop of our signature broth.
